Brand guide

Everything needed to make something that looks like Volvac: the logo, the colours with their values, the one typeface and how to set it, and the rules that keep it together. Social posts and other material should start here.

This is the concept identity prepared by SocialHat. It is not yet approved by Volvac.

Logo

A gauge ring with one live needle, then the name in expanded Archivo. The needle is red, and the ring and the name are one colour: graphite on light grounds, chalk on black. On Volvac red the whole logo is white.

  • On light grounds
  • On Volvac red, all white
  • On black granite
  • Mark alone, for avatars and small spaces
Clear space
Keep a space the width of the ring's stroke times three on every side. Nothing enters it.
Smallest size
Full logo 96px wide on screen. Below that, use the mark alone.
Never
Use the red needle on a red ground, stretch it, outline it, or set the name in another typeface.
Files
Logo graphite PNG, Logo chalk PNG, Mark graphite PNG, Mark chalk PNG, Logo white PNG, Mark white PNG, Avatar on red PNG. SVG versions are in the brand folder and need the Archivo font installed.

Colour

Red is the brand at full size. It starts from the red of Volvac's current logo and is taken a step deeper and less orange, so it holds large areas without glare and keeps white text readable. Navy from the current site is the second colour, with two cool greys and a near-black.

On the website
The homepage is white-led, with red as the signal: white #FFFFFF, soft grey #F1F2F3, graphite text #14171B, mid grey #555D67, Volvac red #C4161F for the logo, buttons and marks, deep red #7E0C13 for pressed states. Red covers about a tenth of a layout, and text on red is always white.
On posts and covers
Solid red grounds are for single strong moments such as a post or a cover. The values below are for those.
  • Volvac red#B0131DThe brand colour. Main ground for covers, hero images and posts. On light grounds it is also the colour of buttons and needles.
  • Deep red#5C0A10The darker red behind and above the red ground, and the text on white buttons.
  • Volvac navy#19385FSecond colour. Details on equipment and in diagrams. Never a full ground.
  • Pale granite#C8CBCBLight ground for product and equipment posts.
  • Lapped steel#DBDFE2Lighter ground for documents and service content.
  • Black granite#0D1012Dark ground for lists.
  • Chalk#F2F5FBText on red and on black. Also buttons and needles on a red ground.
  • Rose chalk#F8D6D6Secondary text on red.
  • Graphite#12161BText on the light grounds.
  • Soft graphite#363E48Secondary text on the light grounds.
  • Ash#A5ACB2Secondary text on black.

Type

One family, Archivo, for everything. It is free from Google Fonts and the variable file is in assets/fonts. The width of the letters does the work a second typeface usually does.

  • Sealed tight.

    HeadlinesArchivo Expanded (width 122 to 125), weight 680 to 700, sentence case, tight line spacing, tracking -2.5%.

  • Vacuum packaging machines, tray sealers, shrink tanks, slicers and tumblers.

    SentencesArchivo at normal width, weight 400, line spacing 1.55.

  • Food processing | Packaging

    Labels and buttonsArchivo Condensed (width 68 to 75), weight 600 to 700, capitals, tracking +9 to +12%. Short phrases only.

  • 0.98 bar

    Numbers and readingsArchivo Condensed (width 70), tabular numerals. Light while a value moves, bold once it settles.

  • Tray sealers

    ListsArchivo Extra Condensed (width 62), weight 700, capitals.

Rules that hold it together

Red leads
Red is the ground of anything that carries the brand. On light and black grounds, red is kept for needles, things you press, and position marks. On a red ground those become chalk. Text on red is always chalk.
One typeface
Archivo only. Capitals with wide tracking are for short condensed labels, never for headlines or sentences.
Machined corners
Corners are 2px. No pills, no rounded cards. Circles only where a real object has one: a dial, a lamp, a punched hole.
Hairlines, not boxes
Separate things with 1px lines in the text colour. No panels, no card grids, no drop shadows on text or shapes.
Real objects
Imagery is the 3D equipment on stone, lit like a workshop. No stock photos, no icons standing in for products.
True claims only
Nothing about customers, certifications or performance that Volvac has not published. Any reading from a 3D model is labelled illustrative.
Plain wording
Say what the machine does in the words an engineer would use. Short sentences. No exclamation marks, no em dashes.

Making a social post

Every post is one ground, one object, one headline. The social posts page renders nine ready examples in portrait 1080 × 1350 and landscape 1200 × 627, and saves each as a PNG.

Ground
Volvac red for brand posts, pale granite for equipment, black granite for lists.
Logo
Top left, chalk on dark grounds and graphite on light ones, with the margin equal on both sides.
Headline
Expanded Archivo, sentence case, three lines at most.
Body
One or two sentences in normal-width Archivo, in the secondary text colour.
Scale
The graduated scale runs down the right edge with one index mark: red on light and black grounds, chalk on red.
Foot
The web address in a condensed label, bottom left.
