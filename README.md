# Volvac

Brand and homepage concept for Volvac, prepared by SocialHat. Not yet an official Volvac site.

- `index.html`: the homepage concept (open it in a browser, no build step).
- `brand.html`: the brand guide. Logo, colours with hex values, type, rules, and how to lay out a social post. Start here for any new material.
- `brand/`: logo and mark files, PNG and SVG.
- `social.html`: nine ready social posts in portrait and landscape, each saved as a PNG from the page.
- `samples.html`: three sample posts in Volvac's current brand (their wordmark, red `#F20000` and navy, from volvac.com.tr), from the same 3D scene. The proposal shows them too; the posts live in `assets/js/samples.js`.
- `proposal/stages-and-costs.html`: stages and costs for the website and the marketing track. Prices are in `PLAN` at the top of `proposal/proposal.js`; print to PDF from Chrome.

Website colours: white `#FFFFFF`, soft grey `#F1F2F3`, graphite `#14171B`, Volvac red `#C4161F`, deep red `#7E0C13`. Post and cover colours: red ground `#B0131D`, deep red `#5C0A10`, navy `#19385F`, pale granite `#C8CBCB`, lapped steel `#DBDFE2`, black granite `#0D1012`, chalk `#F2F5FB`. Typeface: Archivo (in `assets/fonts`).
